# Worked examples — see the flow before you risk your own

Three complete runs, start to finish, on a real-shaped Long Island business.
Each one shows what went IN and exactly what the kit produced from it — nothing
skipped, nothing tidied up afterward.

Read whichever one matches what you want to try. Then do the same thing with
your own business and compare.

| # | File | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `01-wrap-a-session.md` | Ending a day with "I'm done" — including the kit **refusing** a vague log and asking for the real next step |
| 2 | `02-contradiction-check.md` | The owner proposes a job, and the AI catches that it contradicts a decision he made seven weeks ago |
| 3 | `03-monthly-brief.md` | A month of memory rolled into one page he can forward, plus the portable MEMORY BRIEF block |

**The business in all three:** Sorrento Bros. Landscaping — Huntington, NY.
Family-run, three crews, mostly residential, some light commercial. Every
number, town, and quote below is invented for the example; it is shaped like a
real book of business, not taken from one.

**These are examples, not your install.** Your install verifies on YOUR data —
one real fact from your own business — never on anything in this folder. The
installer will ask you for it and wait. If you want to run an example
afterward to poke at it, that is fine; it just never stands in for the real
first run.
