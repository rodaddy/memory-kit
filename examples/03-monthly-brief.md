# Example 3 — A month rolled into one page you can send

Sorrento Bros. Landscaping, 2026-09-01. Tony's brother Vin does the books and
has been out since mid-August. Tony needs to catch him up without a phone call.

---

## What Tony typed

> what changed last month? Vin's been out, I need to get him up to speed

## What the AI came back with

> Rolling up 2026-08-01 through 2026-08-31.

Then it read every August entry in `changelog.md`, `decisions.md`, and
`session-logs/`, plus the standing decisions from before August, and wrote
`briefs/2026-08.md`:

```
# Sorrento Bros. Landscaping — What changed, August 2026
Covering 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-31 · written 2026-09-01

## The short version
- Spring cleanup pricing moved off the flat $285 to size bands; long-time
  customers are grandfathered at the old rate through 2027.
- A fuel surcharge went on every job south of Route 112.
- The Greenlawn HOA came up and is still undecided.
- One thing has been open since 2026-08-04 and needs a call.

## Decisions made this period
- 2026-08-04 — Grandfather repeat customers of 2+ years at $285 through the
  2027 season — "losing a 10-year customer over $140 costs more than the $140."
- 2026-08-19 — Fuel surcharge applies to the whole job, not per visit —
  simpler to explain to a customer than a line that moves every week.

## Changes made this period
- 2026-08-04 — Spring cleanup moved from flat $285 to size bands: under 1/4
  acre $285, 1/4-1/2 acre $425, over 1/2 acre quoted on site (was flat $285) —
  the flat rate lost money on every property over a half acre.
- 2026-08-19 — Added a $12 fuel surcharge on jobs south of Route 112 (was
  none) — diesel and the drive time down there were coming out of the margin.

## Still open
- Does the grandfathered $285 apply to a repeat customer who moved to a bigger
  property? Open since 2026-08-04 — four weeks, and the first one of these
  will show up in the spring.
- Greenlawn HOA (40 units, off-route): pricing it, not decided. Open since
  2026-08-28.

## Where things stand now
Cleanup is priced by size instead of one flat number, which fixes the money
lost on the big Dix Hills properties without pushing away the long-time
customers, who stay at $285 through next season. Jobs south of Route 112 carry
a $12 surcharge for the drive. The one real hole is the grandfathering edge
case, which has sat four weeks. Greenlawn is a live question and is being
priced, not committed to.
```

## The reversals section

Because two August decisions touched older ones, the brief also carried:

```
## Reversals and second thoughts
- The 2026-08-04 size-band pricing replaces the flat $285 that had stood since
  spring 2025. What changed: the Dix Hills half-acre-plus properties.
- The Greenlawn HOA question (2026-08-28) revisits the 2026-07-22
  residential-first rule and the Brookhaven contract dropped 2026-06-30.
```

Nothing else in the kit produces that section, and it is usually the page worth
reading twice.

## Then, the forwardable version

> Want this as a page you can send someone, or is the note enough?

Tony:

> a page, Vin won't open a text file

It wrote `briefs/2026-08.html` beside it: one self-contained file, all styling
inline, Sorrento Bros. at the top in the green from `brand.md`, sections laid
out with room to breathe. Opens by double-clicking, works with no internet, and
prints clean to a PDF. Tony emailed it.

> August brief saved — 2 decisions, 2 changes, 2 still open (one since
> 2026-08-04). The HTML page is beside it.

## The portable block

Later that week Tony was out on a job with only his phone and no access to the
memory folder, and asked: *"give me everything so I can paste it into ChatGPT
on my phone."*

```
MEMORY BRIEF — Sorrento Bros. Landscaping — as of 2026-09-05

STANDING DECISIONS
- 2026-08-19 — Fuel surcharge applies to the whole job, not per visit — easier
  to explain to a customer than a number that moves weekly.
- 2026-08-04 — Repeat customers of 2+ years grandfathered at $285 cleanup
  through the 2027 season — losing a 10-year customer over $140 costs more.
- 2026-07-22 — Residential-first; commercial only inside the existing route —
  off-route commercial cost two residential stops each.
- 2026-06-30 — 50% deposit on any job over $2,000, no exceptions.

RECENT CHANGES
- 2026-08-19 — $12 fuel surcharge on jobs south of Route 112 (was none).
- 2026-08-04 — Spring cleanup moved to size bands: under 1/4 acre $285,
  1/4-1/2 acre $425, over 1/2 acre quoted on site (was flat $285).

WHERE WE LEFT OFF
- Price the Greenlawn HOA (40 units, off-route) before deciding it.

STILL OPEN
- Grandfathered $285 for a repeat customer who moved to a bigger property —
  open since 2026-08-04.
- Greenlawn HOA decision — open since 2026-08-28.
```

> Paste this at the top of any chat, with any AI, and it'll know where you
> stand. It's your memory — you can take it anywhere, including away from this
> setup.

---

## What to notice

1. **Every line traces to a dated entry.** The brief is a rollup, not new
   opinion. Nothing appears in it that is not already in the memory.
2. **The stale item is called out with its age.** Four weeks open. Tidying that
   away would have made a prettier page and a useless one.
3. **The reasoning survives the summary.** "Moved to size bands" is a fact;
   "the flat rate lost money over a half acre" is the part worth keeping.
4. **It is genuinely portable.** The MEMORY BRIEF block runs in any AI with no
   kit installed. That is deliberate — this is Tony's paper, not a hostage.
