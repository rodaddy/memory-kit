# Start Here — Memory Kit

You downloaded a zip. Here is the one thing to do: get the file **installer.md** in front of your AI. Pick your device.

## iPhone / iPad

1. Open the **Files** app and find the zip you downloaded.
2. **Tap the zip** — it unzips into a folder with the same name. Open that folder.
3. Tap **installer.md** to open it, tap the **Share** icon, and share it into your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT). If sharing isn't offered, open installer.md, select all, copy, and paste it into a new chat.

## Windows

1. **Right-click** the zip → **Extract All** → **Extract**.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. **Drag installer.md** into your AI app or the AI's website chat box. (Or open it in Notepad, select all, copy, paste into the chat.)

## Mac

1. **Double-click** the zip — it unzips into a folder next to it.
2. Open the folder.
3. **Drag installer.md** into your AI app or the AI's website chat box.

---

Send that file to your AI and say: **install the memory kit.**
